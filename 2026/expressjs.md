# 15 Years of Node.js: The life and legacy of Express.js

## Short Description

Node.js has turned 15 and Express.js, its humble web framework, still powers millions of APIs and apps worldwide.  
In this talk, we’ll explore how Express shaped the JavaScript backend, what made it so enduring, and what it can teach us about building simple, resilient software in a fast-moving ecosystem.

## Abstract

In 2009, Node.js redefined server-side development. Shortly after, Express.js gave developers a minimal, elegant way to build APIs and web apps, and it’s still here, 15 years later.  
This session takes you through the evolution of Express and the Node.js ecosystem: from callbacks to async/await, CommonJS to ESM, and monoliths to microservices.  
We’ll discuss how Express survived waves of frameworks, what patterns have stood the test of time, and what’s next for the Node ecosystem.  
This is a story about open source endurance, about how simplicity, stability, and community collaboration outlast trends.

## What the Audience Will Gain

- A deeper understanding of **Express.js design principles** and why they aged well  
- Insights into **Node.js evolution** — from 0.10 to today’s modern runtime  
- Lessons in **maintaining long-lived open source projects**  
- Practical takeaways for **designing APIs and frameworks built to last**  
- A vision of where **Node.js is headed in its next decade**

## Target Audience

Backend and full-stack engineers, open source contributors, Node.js enthusiasts, and engineering leaders interested in software longevity.

## Level  

All levels — technical depth for practitioners, historical insights for everyone.

## Suggested Duration

30–45 minutes (with optional live demo or historical code walk)
